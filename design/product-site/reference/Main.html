Terminal output that survives the browser.

runemark renders ANSI streams — status, reports, diffs, errors — as accessible, script-free HTML. Same renderer in Rust and Node.

Captured once, readable everywhere.

runemark sits between the command that prints colours and the page that shows them. Nothing runs in the reader's browser.
